Our Mission

Built by students, for firefighters.

Southlake Project Pulmonary is a student-led chapter dedicated to assisting firefighters with pulmonary rehabilitation and raising awareness for lung health through outreach, service, and advocacy. Our goal is to help our community, empower our members, and build a strong, sustainable presence that will impact our community in the long term.
