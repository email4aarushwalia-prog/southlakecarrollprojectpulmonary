Frequently Asked

Common questions

Is this a big time commitment?+

The time commitment is whatever you choose it to be. We don't require a set number of events or meetings; participate whenever you want.

Do I need prior experience?+

No. Anyone interested in making a difference is welcome. We provide training and guidance about pulmonary health, advocacy, and service.

Am I required to attend every meeting or event?+

No. We encourage involvement, but you participate whenever your schedule allows. Every contribution helps.

Will I receive volunteer hours?+

Yes! Members receive volunteer hours for every initiative they attend.

How can I get a leadership position?+

Leadership opens around April. The best way in is consistent engagement, bringing friends to meetings, and attending as many initiatives as possible.

Who can join?+

Any Southlake Carroll student (grades 9–12) passionate about community service, public health, and supporting firefighters is welcome.
