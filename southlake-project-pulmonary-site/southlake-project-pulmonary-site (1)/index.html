Our Mission

Standing behind the people who breathe for us.

Southlake Project Pulmonary is dedicated to assisting firefighters with pulmonary rehabilitation and raising awareness for lung health through outreach, service, and advocacy. We help our community, empower our members, and build a strong, sustainable presence for the long term.

Research

Understand the Risk

Firefighters face repeated exposure to smoke, toxic particulates, and airborne carcinogens. We study the science so our advocacy is grounded in fact.

Advocacy

Push for Policy

We meet with city board members and policymakers to push for real support for firefighter respiratory health.

Service

Show Up, Every Month

From hydration drives to care package assembly, we host 1–3 events or fundraisers every month of the school year.

Active Right Now

Breathe for 343

This September, Project Pulmonary chapters across the country honor the 343 firefighters lost on September 11, 2001, by assembling and delivering hydration kits to local fire stations.
