What we're building this year

From national tribute campaigns to monthly community drives, here's how we turn advocacy into action.

Live Now · Aug–Sept

Breathe for 343 Hydration Kit Campaign

Project Pulmonary chapters nationwide honor the 343 firefighters lost on September 11, 2001, by assembling and delivering hydration kits to local fire stations. This campaign recognizes the sacrifices firefighters continue to make every day while promoting wellness and respiratory health.

First Semester · Subject to Change

Month-by-Month Plan

August

Spreading advocacy and awareness

  • Start recruiting members and establishing meeting times
  • Host the first chapter meetings
  • Create presentations regarding advocacy
  • Start contacting fire stations, nonprofits, and community leaders
  • Start working on the Breathless 9/11 Campaign
  • Post weekly educational content
September

Launching service initiatives

  • Host a school-wide hydration drive
  • Figure out two events to host
  • Recruit volunteers
  • Set up awareness tables at events and school
  • Establish 1–2 partnerships for an advocacy project
  • Donate hydration materials to fire stations
October

Educating on lung health

  • Organize a Letters for Lungs event
  • Host a lung health awareness booth
  • Host one fundraiser for pulmonary rehabilitation organizations
  • Invite guest speakers to meetings, events, and fundraisers
  • Launch a lung health social media campaign
November

Supporting firefighters directly

  • Organize a firefighter care package drive
  • Recruit volunteers and grant hours
  • Secure sponsorships from local businesses
  • Run 2–3 fundraising events for specific causes
  • Assemble care packages
  • Publish a blog highlighting our progress
December

Expanding advocacy & outreach

  • Launch a lung health awareness campaign (3–4 events)
  • Create a digital lung health resource guide
  • Present findings to the city council
  • Publish a semester impact report
  • Continue educational blog posts
Our Year-Long Goals

What we're working toward

Recognition

Receiving a chapter spotlight from the official Project Pulmonary and recognition from our local city board.

Growth

Growing to 15–30+ active members and creating partnerships with local fire stations, nonprofits, and organizations.

Consistency

Hosting at least 1–3 events or fundraisers every month while spreading advocacy for pulmonary rehabilitation.
